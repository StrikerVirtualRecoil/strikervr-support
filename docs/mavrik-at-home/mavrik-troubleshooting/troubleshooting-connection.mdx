# Troubleshooting Connection

## Mavrik

### Bluetooth Connection Failed or Blaster Not Recognized

1. Power cycle the blaster by powering it off, waiting 10 seconds, and powering it on.
2. Press and hold the **L / R side buttons**, located above the trigger/finger guard, until the LED strip blinks and then turns off.

   :::note
   The blaster looks powered off after this step because the LEDs are turned off, but the blaster is still on.
   :::

3. Power cycle the blaster again.
4. Try to reconnect to the headset.

### Unable to Pair to New Device After Connecting to One Previous

1. Forget the Mavrik from the originally connected device/headset.
2. Power off the originally connected device.
3. Press and hold the **L / R side buttons**, located above the trigger/finger guard, until the LED strip blinks and then turns off.
4. Power cycle the blaster by powering it off, waiting 10 seconds, and powering it on.
5. Try connecting to the new headset.

## Headset

### Failed StrikerConnect Installation

1. Power cycle your headset by powering it off, waiting 10 seconds, and powering it on.
2. Try to reinstall StrikerConnect. See the [Mavrik Quick Start Guide](/docs/mavrik-at-home/mavrik-setup/mavrik-quick-start-guide) for installation instructions.

### LED Strip is Not Solid/Not Connecting to StrikerConnect

1. Forget the Mavrik from the headset Bluetooth.
2. Power cycle both headset and Mavrik by powering them off, waiting 10 seconds, and powering them on.
3. Reconnect the Mavrik to the headset via Bluetooth.
